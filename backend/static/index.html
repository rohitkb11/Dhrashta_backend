How to interpret these measures

Totals cover retained alerts across uploads and external sensors. High severity means confidence ≥ 85%; medium is 70–84.9%; low is below 70%. These bands reflect the detector score, not verified impact. BENIGN is included in totals and excluded from the threat heatmap. Input receipt and dashboard connectivity do not prove full network visibility.

The charts below use the recent 100 detections. The heatmap uses retained alerts from the last seven UTC dates. Endpoint evidence comes from reported addresses or linked input observations; missing and ambiguous addresses stay unknown.
